3. Mathematical Formulation of the CP-SAT Optimizer

The optimizer schedules aircraft maintenance tasks across a 30-day horizon ($H = 30$). For each aircraft $i \in \{1, \dots, 24\}$, let $S_i$ be the maintenance start day, $d_i$ the duration, $E_i = S_i + d_i$ the end day, and $b_{i,j} \in \{0, 1\}$ the assignment to hangar bay $j \in \{\text{Bay-1, Bay-2, Bay-3}\}$.

Objective Function (Maximize Total Ready Aircraft-Days):
$$\max \sum_{i=1}^{24} \left( 25 \cdot \text{IsScheduled}_i - 12 \cdot \text{IsLate}_i - 1 \cdot S_i \right)$$ Subject to Hard Operational Constraints:
1. Pessimistic RUL Safety Deadline: $S_i \le \text{SafeDays}_i - d_i$ (enforced with late penalty $L_i$ if violated)
2. Spares Lead-Time Arrival: $S_i \ge \text{SpareLeadTime}_i + \text{SupplyLag}$
3. Hangar Bay No-Overlap: $\text{AddNoOverlap}\left(\{ [S_i, E_i] \mid b_{i,j} = 1 \}\right) \quad \forall j \in \{\text{Bay 1, 2, 3}\}$
4. Technician Roster Cumulative Limits: $\text{AddCumulative}\left(\{ [S_i, E_i, \text{Techs}_i] \}, \text{DailyTechCap}\right)$
5. Work Bundling: Consolidated single bay entry per airframe visit.
